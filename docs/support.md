# Support

Amiberry is developed and supported by Dimitris Panokostas (BlitterStudio). This page is for every version: iPhone, iPad and Mac from the App Store, Android from Google Play, and the builds for Linux, macOS, Windows, FreeBSD and Haiku.

## Getting Started

- [Quick Start Guide](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/wiki/Quick-start-guide)
- [Kickstart ROMs](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/wiki/Kickstart-ROMs-(BIOS)): Amiberry includes the free AROS replacement ROM, so it boots without your own Kickstart ROM. For best compatibility, use Kickstart ROMs you own.
- [WHDLoad Workflow](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/wiki/WHDLoad-Workflow)
- [Frequently Asked Questions](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/wiki/FAQ) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/wiki/Troubleshooting)
- The full documentation is in the [Amiberry wiki](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/wiki).

## Reporting a Problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry/issues). Please include:

- your device and system version (for example "iPad Pro 13-inch, iPadOS 27.1");
- the Amiberry version (shown on the About & Licences screen);
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- the machine model and, if possible, the configuration you used.

Please don't attach Kickstart ROMs or commercial software: their licences don't allow sharing them.
